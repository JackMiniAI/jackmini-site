# Local Map Check Electronic-Only Outreach Playbook

Updated: 2026-06-17

## Decision

Cold email stays in the mix, but not as a one-shot pitch to buy the audit.

The new job of cold outreach is:
- get a reply
- earn permission
- deliver proof
- then route interested prospects to `jackmini.com/local-edge`

## Why the old version underperformed

- It asked for too much trust on touch one
- It tried to sell the audit before earning engagement
- It relied too heavily on one channel and one touch
- Outbound identity was not clean enough

## Channel rules

Electronic communications only:
- email
- website contact forms
- LinkedIn DM only if the profile is clearly the owner or decision-maker and the account is easy to verify

Do not use:
- phone
- voicemail
- SMS
- calendar links

## Sequence

### Touch 1

Goal: earn a tiny reply.

Use:
- one clear observation
- one simple impact line
- one CTA: reply `audit`

Do not include:
- pricing
- long credential dump
- meeting ask

### Touch 2

Send 3-4 business days later if no reply.

Goal: restate relevance, add one more proof point, keep the same tiny CTA.

Use:
- second finding
- same CTA: reply `audit`

### Touch 3

Send 5-7 business days after touch 2 if no reply.

Goal: close the loop without sounding needy.

Use:
- short final note
- same CTA: reply `audit`

## Prospect qualification

Prioritize businesses where all are true:
- weak or incomplete Google Maps signals are obvious
- website and Maps listing clearly mismatch
- the service value is high enough that one extra customer matters
- there is a visible owner or decision-maker email, or at least a real contact form

Deprioritize:
- listings with already strong review velocity
- generic front-desk inboxes when no better path exists
- businesses with weak websites and weak Maps pages if the owner is likely too unsophisticated to value the audit

## Vertical position

Best current fit:
- attorneys
- dentists
- CPAs

Next tests, only if current sequence still fails:
- PT clinics
- HVAC
- chiropractors

## Success metric

Primary metric:
- reply rate to touch 1 + touch 2

Secondary metrics:
- positive reply rate
- audit-page clicks after interest
- conversion from interested reply to paid audit

## Approval boundary

Internal research, lead prep, draft creation, CSV prep, and cron updates are autonomous.

Any outbound send still needs Alex approval.
